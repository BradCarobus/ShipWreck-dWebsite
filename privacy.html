Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Overview

Ship Wreck'd ("the app") does not collect, store, or share any personal data or usage information from its players. You can play the app without creating an account and without any data leaving your device.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personally identifiable information, device identifiers, location data, analytics, or usage data. The app does not include third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

Children's Privacy

Because Ship Wreck'd does not collect any personal information from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If our data practices change in the future, this policy will be updated and the new version will be posted on this page.
